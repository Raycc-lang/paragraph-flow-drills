**Bottleneck** — pick one, don't invent a new phrasing each time, or the column
becomes unsortable:

- `unpaired` — practiced with no correct output available
- `fluency-substituted` — repeated known material where novel was needed
- `recognition-only` — analyzed but never produced
- `over-correction` — D9, damaged working prose
- `wrong-layer` — treated a D8 coherence failure as a flow problem
- `sentence-level` — the errors were below the paragraph
- `none` — clean session

## Review cadence

**Every 10 sessions**, answer these four in writing. This is the step-7 review the
project is required to carry, and it is expected to rewrite the plan.

1. Which defect codes recur? Are they the same as ten sessions ago?
2. What is the Mode E ratio? If under a third, why?
3. Has any clean item been marked defective? How many?
4. Does the evidence still support this target, or has the bottleneck moved?

**Every 20 sessions**, re-run the §0 diagnostic with fresh briefs and compare
against the original. Two paragraphs written cold, timed. That comparison is the
only real answer to "is this working."
