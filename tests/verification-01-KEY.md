## Part 1

### V1 — SOUND
Linear progression. `figures` → `The figures` → `The overrun` → `the overrun`. Each
sentence's new information becomes the next departure point. Nothing to repair.

### V2 — SOUND (trap item)
Contrastive progression. Sentence 2 opens with entirely new information — the
reversal is the point, and `held up better` / `collapsed` makes the contrast
structural without a connective. Sentence 3 then uses split progression on `Both`.

Calling this defective is a boundary error and scores −1.

**Design note:** this item shares its structure with G5 in the starter corpus. So
do V4 (with G11/T4), V5 (with G8/T5), and V7 (with G3). That makes this test a
measure of **near transfer** — the same distinction applied to new content —
which is worth knowing but is not evidence of generalization. Getting V2 right
after G5 may mean you learned the structure, or may mean you recognized the
template. This test cannot tell the two apart. `verification-02.md` can.

### V3 — DEFECTIVE: D7, and arguably D3
`Moreover` marks addition. The real relation is concession — record rainfall *and
yet* reservoirs are low *and so* restrictions remain. Two relations are being
flattened into one wrong marker.

*One repair:* `Rainfall in the catchment was the highest on record for March. Even
so, reservoir levels remain below the seasonal average, and the restrictions
announced in February stay in force.`

Note what changed: not "a transition was added." A wrong relation was replaced
with the right one, and the third clause was subordinated to show it follows from
the second rather than standing alongside it.

### V4 — SOUND (trap item)
Instruction-first. The imperative leads because acting before finishing the
paragraph is costly — the serial number becomes unreadable once the unit is
mounted. Given-before-new is correctly overridden by hazard-first convention. Same
override as G11 and T4.

### V5 — DEFECTIVE: D8 (coherence)
Post hoc reasoning. `Therefore` claims that one component of a programme is the
active ingredient, which the stated evidence cannot support — schools that adopt a
programme differ in other ways, and the programme does other things.

**Correct response is refusal.** If you repaired this with flow work, score −1.

### V6 — SOUND
Derived topics from `three areas`, with a split development. Three announced,
three delivered, in an order the second sentence justifies (`because the
outstanding complaints concerned it`). The justification is what makes the
ordering non-arbitrary.

### V7 — DEFECTIVE: D1, D5
- **D1** — `This has been disputed` has at least two antecedents: the break clause,
  or the fact that the lease sets it out. The dispute described in the rest of the
  sentence suggests a third reading — the agreement's date. Genuinely ambiguous.
- **D5** — `The tenancy agreement` / `The lease` / `the contract` / `the document`:
  four names, one referent, in three sentences. In this register that is not
  variety, it is a reader burden.

## Part 2 — what a good repair explanation looks like

Not: *"this flows better now."*

But: *"`Moreover` told the reader the second fact was additional support for the
first. It isn't — it cuts against it. A reader tracking the argument would have
had to re-read to discover the marker was wrong, and some readers would simply
have absorbed the wrong relation and moved on."*

Every change names what the reader could not do before.

## Part 3 — grading your own production

Score against the seven-item rubric in the test file. Two additional checks the
rubric doesn't cover:

**P1** — this brief has a hidden requirement: the reader `needs to be able to
decide whether to escalate to their manager`. A paragraph that explains the cause
beautifully and never makes the decision actionable fails item 6 regardless of how
it reads. Check whether you told them what the decision is and what it costs.

**P2** — the reader `needs to be able to explain it to someone else afterwards`.
That is a harder bar than "understands it." It requires the explanation to have a
retainable shape — usually one mechanism, named, rather than three factors listed.
Check whether yours is repeatable from memory.
